# 5kg Center Spool Holder


This works extremely well with almost no friction.

Print two stands, bearing hubs, and spool hubs and one axle.  You will need two 6204-2RS bearings, you can get them on Amazon (https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07BN3CW22).

The bottom stand has a lot of friction and the printer may have issues pulling hard enough to unspool.

Spool holder and bearing roller for 5kg spool.  This was specifically designed for the measurements of a Polymaker 5kg spool, but I have no reason to believe it is not generic.  This takes the pin and bearings from the 1kg spool holder: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CCTQYJZ2

Remove the pin and bearing from the following spool holder: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CCTQYJZ2
You have to make two of these for each side of the spool
